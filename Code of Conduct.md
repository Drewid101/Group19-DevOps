# **<u>Team Code of Conduct</u>** 

This Code of Conduct sets the expectations for each team member for the duration of the trimester. It supplements the Edinburgh Napier Community Code. It exists to encourage a positive, collaborative environment where every member feels valued and heard, while ensuring the successful achievement of the course and project objectives. 

1. Respect and Dignity 

   - Disagreement is fine; disrespect isn’t. Every team member will respect differing viewpoints and opinions. 

   - Provide constructive criticism of the work, not the person. Likewise, accept feedback on your work with grace and an open mind. 

   - Harassment of any kind, based on but not limited to nationality, race, gender or age, whether through designated team communication channels, or private interactions, will not be tolerated and will be treated as stated in 5a of this code. 

   - Members will practice understanding as it regards the time zone, personal commitments, and language proficiency of fellow team members. 

   - Members will respect the time, effort, and contributions made by others toward the project's success. 

   - All interactions should contribute to a respectful, inclusive, and professional team environment. 

2. Communication 

   - Microsoft Teams will be the primary means of communication. All project-related information should be shared through this channel unless otherwise agreed. 

   - Efforts should be made to keep all team members informed about progress, responsibilities, decisions and deadlines. 

   - Avoid being a passive member. Share your thoughts, ideas, and opinions regarding the project tasks and decisions. 

   - Attendance in the team standups, along with active participation in the designated Team channel, is expected unless prior notice is given. 

   - Members should indicate as early as possible if they are unavailable to attend a standup. 

   - Team members participating in stand-up meetings will ensure meetings are recorded, and that key decisions are documented and shared with the entire team. 

   - Members are to communicate the progress, success or challenges of their assigned task in a timely manner. 

3. Teamwork 

- “Each one, teach one.” Members with knowledge or expertise on a topic should assist and support teammates where possible. 

- Do not hesitate to ask for help when needed. Seeking assistance promotes learning, collaboration, and project success. 

- Be supportive of each other and maintain a positive solution focused attitude. Avoid unnecessary negativity or emotional waste. 

- Team members should strive to produce high-quality work and support one another in maintaining project standards. 

- Major project decisions should be agreed by the whole team and should be made on the best interest of the project. 

- There shall be no hierarchy within the team. Every member's opinion deserves consideration. 

- The team will fairly acknowledge and recognize the contributions of all members. 

## 4. Individual Accountability 

- Members will be honest about their contribution and will not take credit for someone else’s work. 

- Each member is responsible for completing their tasks by the agreed deadlines. 

- Members will strive to produce the highest quality work they can deliver. 

- Members should make every effort to be present and prepared for code reviews, sprint reviews, and other relevant project activities. 

- Team members will commit to continuously developing their skills, knowledge, and understanding throughout the project. 

- Members will take ownership of their responsibilities and proactively address any issues that may affect their ability to contribute 

- Members should indicate early if they are having difficulty completing a task or keeping up with the course. 

## 5. Breach of the Code 

- a) Misconduct and Harassment – Any form of harassment, discrimination or disrespectful behaviour will be addressed promptly. Actions taken will be determined by the severity of the breach. 

   - The issue may be raised and discussed with the team. 

   - If unresolved, the issue will be documented and the delivery team module informed. 

   - Further escalation to the Edinburgh Napier Complaints Team can be sought. 

- b) Failure to Contribute- Members who consistently fail to communicate, attend meetings, complete assigned tasks, or contribute meaningfully to the project may be subject to: 

   - Review by the team during Stand-ups 

   - Redistribution or removal of responsibilities. 

   - Formal documentation of non-participation and notification to the module delivery team. 

 Dismissal from the group by the module delivery team
